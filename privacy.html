Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how BoostArk ("the app") handles information when its owner connects their YouTube channels.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google OAuth, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to read channel and video information, comments and analytics, and to upload and update videos on the owner's own channels.

How data is used

Data is used only to show the owner their channel information and to carry out the actions the owner requests. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and channel data are stored locally on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any BoostArk server.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions. Deleting the app's local data removes everything it stored.

Google API Services

BoostArk's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
